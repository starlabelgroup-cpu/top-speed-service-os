Who it’s for

Any Top Speed customer — your referral code is issued automatically when a job completes.

How it works

  1. Create your free account with the sign-up form on this page and sign the account terms.
  2. Verify your email — we send one verification link; it takes one click and confirms the address your confirmations go to.
  3. Complete a job with Top Speed and your personal referral code is issued automatically.
  4. Share your code. When a friend’s job is completed and paid, you earn service credit: $25 for each of your first referrals, $35 each from your 5th, and $50 each from your 10th — plus a one-time $100 bonus at 10 lifetime referrals and $250 at 25. Credits expire 12 months after the day they are issued.
Full program rules live on the Refer & Earn page. Credits are service credit toward future Top Speed service — and they are a different $25 from the New Customer Welcome discount your friend gets on their first repair.
