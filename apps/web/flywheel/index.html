    Book OS — Service Setters dispatch

    Ads only work if somebody answers, confirms, and dispatches fast. Book OS is the appointment-setting and dispatch layer inside Top Speed Service OS — powered by the Service Setters team, who quality-check every request before a provider ever rolls.

    1

    Universal intake

    Every lead — both domains, landing pages, Google Form, phone, flyer QR — lands in one queue with its source tag and a TS- reference code.

    2

    Setters confirm

    Service Setters verify the issue, the address, and the window, and collect the $99 diagnostic — credited toward the repair.

    3

    Match & approve

    The system matches the job to a network provider by area, appliance, and availability. A Setter approves before dispatch — no blind auto-sends.

    4

    Live tracking

    Customer and provider get updates at every step: booked → dispatched → en route → arrived, by text.

    5

    Complete & trigger

    Job completion closes the loop and automatically fires the review request and the customer’s referral code — feeding Stages 4 and 5.

    Why it matters for ads: a confirmed, prepaid appointment converts ad spend into scheduled work instead of missed calls — and every completion restarts the flywheel with a review and a referral.

    Flywheel Operating Rules

    The non-negotiables that keep the loop honest and measurable.

    1. Every campaign points to a booking surfaceNo ad, post, flyer, or QR code ends anywhere except a page that can take a booking or a qualified lead.
    2. Every lead is source-taggedThe Service OS intake records where each request came from — search, LSA, Meta, GBP, flyer QR, referral, phone — under one TS- reference code.
    3. Service Setters confirm before dispatchHybrid dispatch: the system matches, a human Setter approves. Speed without losing the quality check.
    4. Completion always triggers Review + ReferThe review request and referral code fire automatically when a job closes — never left to memory.
    5. Savings loop back to AttractWork won through reviews and referrals lowers what the next job costs to win; that margin funds the channels at Stage 1.
    Budgets are suggestions only. The approved marketing pack illustrates roughly ~$50/day for Google and a ~$20–30/day Meta test as starting examples — planning figures to decide on, not current spend.
