Field Pro — the work-order workspace

Every assignment from Service Setters dispatch opens as a full field workspace in the Tech App:

  • Today's jobs and new offers — accept or decline for real
  • Job steps that mirror the customer's live tracking
  • A service checklist saved to the job record
  • Tech notes — typed, or dictated and structured by Speedy, always confirmed by you

Module 2 · Appliance Plug

Appliance Plug — snap & order parts

Built into every Field Pro workspace, and available as its own app:

  • Snap the model plate — Speedy transcribes brand, model, and serial
  • You confirm every field before anything is ordered
  • Parts Finder returns part categories with fitment and verify-first notes
  • Order drafts carry the job's TS reference automatically
  • Routed Appliance Plus Parts Supply first, other distributors second, through Parts Supply OS
  • Statuses move only on a real tap: Draft → Sent → Ordered → Received

Who it's for

The Top Speed Field Team and vetted network providers. Jobs arrive through Service Setters dispatch — Speedy Pro is the tool you run them with, not a lead marketplace.

Speedy helps. You decide.

Speedy reads plates, drafts notes, and lines up part categories. The technician confirms everything: Speedy never orders on its own, never certifies a repair's safety, and never changes a job status by itself.

Honest parts ordering

Parts Finder works in categories on purpose — exact OEM part numbers and prices are confirmed by Parts Supply OS at order time, against the confirmed model, never guessed from a photo.
