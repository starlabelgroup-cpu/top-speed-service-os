The Platform

Everything you need to grow.

One advertising ecosystem for businesses, agencies and publishers — built into the same Service OS that takes the bookings.

AI Campaign Studio

Speedy, the platform's AI assistant, drafts headlines, copy and campaign concepts in seconds. Nothing goes live on its own — every draft waits in an approval queue for a human yes.

In-House Display Network

Your campaigns broadcast from the Ad Studio hub across Top Speed's own websites and apps — one setup, every surface, served and rotated by the Service OS itself.

Every Channel, One Board

Google, Meta, Microsoft and your other external campaigns are tracked on a single readiness board inside Ad Studio — what is live, what is paused, and exactly what each one still needs.

Attribution & the Loop View

Every serve and every click is counted, and first-touch attribution follows the customer to the booking form. The Loop view shows the whole path: serves → clicks → bookings.

Streaming & Video Placements

Streaming-format ads are built into the studio today. Online video, connected TV and audio placements expand through our provider partners — Kevel, Google Ad Manager, SpringServe and FreeWheel — as each one comes online.

Plans & Billing by Stripe

Subscriptions, invoices and plan changes run through Stripe in the Advertiser Console. Media spend is always separate, itemized, and capped by the budget you set.
