CocoCane Express

The flex-warehouse console for CocoCane: ONE catalog, stock held flexibly across the warehouse, the juice shop and the market vehicle — any location can fulfill any channel. Order requests reserve stock the moment they land; cancelling releases it, fulfilling consumes it. No payment is ever taken here — orders are requests, paid at pickup; wholesale is a quote flow.

Staff sign-in required

CocoCane Express is operations data — stock, orders and the wholesale desk are staff-only. Sign in with your Service OS staff account. Nothing here is visible signed out.

Order queue

Retail orders arrive already reserved at their fulfillment location. Wholesale requests arrive unreserved — build the lines, assign a location, then Confirm takes the reservation. Cancel releases stock back; Fulfilled consumes it.

New staff order

Phone and walk-up orders. With items and a location, stock reserves immediately.

Stock by location

On hand, reserved by open orders, and available (on hand − reserved) at every location. A row flags when available hits zero, or at/below the product's staff-set low-stock mark.

Recent stock movements

Transfer stock between locations

Transfers move AVAILABLE stock only (reserved units stay put) and are recorded on both sides — the network total never changes in a transfer.

Adjust stock (count correction)

A signed adjustment to on-hand at one location, always with a reason. On-hand can never drop below what open orders have reserved.

Catalog

One shared catalog for every channel. Prices are CocoCane's locked menu — a fixed price, or a by-size range for sized coconuts. Avocado stays OFF the catalog until it has a locked price.

Stock locations

Locations are data — add a market stall, a second van, a partner shop. Codes are permanent once created.
