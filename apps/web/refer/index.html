Questions

How much is the credit?

You earn a $25 service credit for each friend whose job is completed and paid. From your 5th through your 9th completed referral the credit rises to $35 each, and from your 10th on it's $50 each — plus a one-time $100 bonus at 10 lifetime referrals and $250 at 25. Every credit is approved by our team before it lands, and you'll see its exact amount and expiry in Appliance Connect.

When do I get my credit?

After your friend's job is completed and a staff member approves the referral. You'll see it move through the stages — invited, booked, completed, approved, issued — in your referral dashboard.

Where is my referral code?

Your code is issued automatically when a job completes. Sign in to Appliance Connect and open Refer & Earn to copy your code and your personal link.

What does my friend get?

A welcome offer on their first eligible repair: $25 off your first eligible repair — for new registered customers. Minimum service charge and exclusions apply.

Does my credit expire?

Yes — each issued credit expires 12 months after the day it's issued. Your dashboard shows the exact date for each credit so nothing surprises you.

Can I refer a business?

Yes — businesses and property managers refer on the same program, on the same approved terms, through the partner track. Your partner referral code and your referral stats live where you already work: Appliance Pro for businesses, Appliance Connect Pro for property managers. Same credit tiers, same staff approval, no separate partner rate.
