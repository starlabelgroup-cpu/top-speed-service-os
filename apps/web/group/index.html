THE PLATFORM / TOP SPEED SERVICE OS

One platform under every division.

The Top Speed Service OS is one modular platform that maintains customer, property, equipment, technician, work-order and billing records — with role-based access and division-specific accounting.

MODULE

Booking OS

Web and phone intake, eligibility, service zones, availability and appointment confirmations.

MODULE

Dispatch OS

Skills-based assignment, queue management, technician status, arrival tracking and exception alerts.

MODULE

Technician OS

Diagnosis notes, photos, parts, estimates, customer approvals, checklists and completion.

MODULE

Customer CRM

Service history, appliance profiles, communication preferences, offers and follow-up.

MODULE

Property OS

Multi-site assets, approvals, recurring maintenance and consolidated reporting.

MODULE

Finance OS

Estimates, invoices, deposits, payment links, refunds and payout reconciliation.

MODULE

Inventory OS

SKU catalogs, part compatibility, purchasing, warehouse stock and replenishment.

MODULE

Analytics OS

Conversion, first-time fix rate, utilization, callbacks, gross margin and retention.

Speedy AI — assistance with human oversight

Speedy is the group’s AI agent suite: customer intake, FAQs and status updates; dispatch suggestions based on skills, availability and location; technician references and job history; parts lookup with verification before ordering; and business summaries for the team. Human operators approve safety-critical recommendations, regulated work, estimates and consequential customer decisions — Speedy suggests, people decide.

THE ROADMAP / PHASED BY DESIGN

Growth in phases, earned in order.

The direction of the enterprise, in sequence — each phase is validated by the one before it before capital follows. This is a roadmap, not a schedule of promises.

01

Validate the core

Prove the appliance operation first: licensing and coverage confirmed, lead tracking measured, cost per lead, booking rate, completed jobs and callbacks all visible.

02

Operational software

Role-based customer, dispatcher, technician and admin portals on the Service OS — with payments, notifications and service histories connected.

03

Commercial growth

Property managers, restaurants and commercial service agreements; Appliance Connect Pro piloted with vetted partner coverage and validated parts sourcing.

04

Multi-trade & regional expansion

Qualified trade partners under documented operating standards — expanding only into areas with adequate demand, supervision and unit economics.

05

Platform commercialization

Service Setters outsourcing, Top Speed Digital subscriptions and APIs tested with outside contractors, once compliance and support systems mature.
