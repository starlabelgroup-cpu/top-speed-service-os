The real cost of waiting

Downtime costs more than the repair

A failed appliance in a home is an inconvenience. In a business, it's a meter running — spoiled product, turned customers, idle staff, health-code risk. That's why commercial work runs on a priority track here, not in line behind residential calls.

Cold chain at risk

A dead walk-in cooler

Every hour above temperature puts your entire inventory — and your health inspection — on the line. Product you throw away tonight costs far more than the compressor repair that could have saved it.

Line down, doors open

A broken laundry line

One extractor down in a laundromat or hotel laundry backs up every load behind it. Customers don't wait — they wash somewhere else, and hotel rooms don't turn over without linen.

Rush hour doesn't pause

A cooking line failure

A range or oven failing at 6 p.m. on a Friday isn't a maintenance issue — it's a lost service, comped meals and a dining room you can't seat. Commercial accounts get dispatched first.

How a commercial work order moves

From “it's down” to one monthly invoice

Every commercial job runs through the Top Speed Service OS — the same platform behind topspeedappliance.net — with Service Setters handling priority dispatch. You always know where your work order stands.

01

You open the request

Call the priority line, tap the QR code on the unit itself, or open a request in your client portal. The unit's full history rides along automatically.

02

Service Setters dispatch

Your request jumps the residential queue. Service Setters confirm the details, match a vetted commercial provider, and approve the dispatch.

03

Vetted provider on site

A commercial-capable provider from the Top Speed network arrives with the unit's brand, model and service history already in hand.

04

Track it live

Status updates as the job moves — dispatched, on site, in repair, complete. No chasing anyone for an update.

05

Photo report

Every visit closes with a photo report: what failed, what was done, what to watch. It files straight into the unit's registry record.

06

One monthly invoice

No per-visit card swipes or scattered receipts. All locations, all work orders — one consolidated invoice each month.

    Join the commercial network

    Commercial accounts mean steadier, scheduled work — agreement clients with registered equipment, recurring preventive maintenance, and repeat work orders instead of one-off calls.     What commercial network work requires

    • Commercial experience — refrigeration, laundry, cooking or sanitation equipment in working businesses, not residential-only.
    • Insurance on file — current liability coverage, verified before your first commercial dispatch.
    • Response capability — you can take priority work orders inside Broward County service windows and keep status updated in the app.
    • Photo-report discipline — every visit closes with photos and notes into the unit's registry record. It's how accounts stay and work keeps coming.
    • Registry accuracy — asset tags, model and serial details recorded on every visit, so the client's equipment history stays complete.
