Speedy Verify — Access Codes & Approvals

Speedy Verify is the Master's access-code system: staff and business accounts sign in with a password plus an 8-digit code that only the Master issues. New staff and business accounts start unverified and wait here. Revoking a code denies sign-in immediately. Codes are stored hashed — a code is shown once, when it is issued.

Tracking Numbers — every surface, one registry

Every phone number and mailbox a Top Speed surface publishes, and what each one brought in over the last 28 days. Calls and texts are counted from the contacts that actually reached the number; bookings are counted from real booking records by the surface they came from — for mailboxes, bookings show at the surface level, because mail is not attributable per address. A surface with no dedicated number yet shows Planned and “Not yet assigned” — no number is ever invented for it. Rows are never deleted here; Retired is the end state.
