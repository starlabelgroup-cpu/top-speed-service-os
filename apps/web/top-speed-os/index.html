The structure

One enterprise, two divisions

Top Speed OS is the parent. Top Speed Service OS runs the service side of the business; Top Speed Sales OS runs the retailing side. Both run on the same platform and the same Service Setters dispatch.

Top Speed Service OS — the service side

Appliance repair and installation across 65 cities in Broward, Palm Beach & Miami-Dade: online booking, Service Setters dispatch, live job tracking in Track My Tech, and a provider network of vetted technicians and repair companies. Nine services, flat published pricing — $99 diagnostic credited toward the repair, $210 flat repair labor, $150 flat installation labor.

The retailing division: the Sales OS console where the team runs the catalog and orders, and the Top Speed Appliances shop where customers buy used appliances online. Inventory is entered by staff — real appliances, real prices, nothing invented.
