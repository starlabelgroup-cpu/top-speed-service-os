Install in under a minute

The Top Speed app is a web app — it installs straight from your browser and takes almost no space on your phone. Pick your device below; we highlighted the one we detected.

🍎 iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add — the Top Speed icon lands on your home screen.
  5. Open it any time to book, track, and manage your service.

🤖 Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install the Top Speed App above when it appears — or tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Confirm Install.
  5. Find Top Speed in your app drawer and on your home screen.
