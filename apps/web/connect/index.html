What Connect does for you

Your garage in the Customer App is powered by Appliance Connect — a real registry on the Top Speed Service OS, not a notes app.

📋 One record per appliance

Register each unit once — fridge, washer, dryer, oven, dishwasher. Brand, model, serial and year stay on your account, saved in the Service OS.

🛠️ History that follows the appliance

Every booking you make shows up next to the appliance it was for. When a tech arrives, they see what was done before — no re-explaining, no guessing.

💬 Speedy already knows your home

Talk to Speedy and it works from your record: your appliances, your city, your history. Booking the next visit takes a sentence, not a form.

⏰ Maintenance, remembered

Filters, vent cleanings, warranty dates — Connect keeps the rhythm of your home's upkeep on the same record your service history lives on.
