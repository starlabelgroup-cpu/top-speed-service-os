Straight answers

Before you apply

Do I pay for leads or marketing?

No. There is no lead-buying on the Top Speed network — no per-lead fees and no shared leads sold to competing companies. Job offers are matched to you by Service Setters based on your service areas and the appliances you service.

Who is Service Setters?

Service Setters is Top Speed's central dispatch team. They review provider applications, verify credentials, and quality-check every customer booking — appliance, issue, photos, address, window, and the collected $99 diagnostic — before it becomes a job offer in the app. That's the "hybrid" in hybrid dispatch: platform matching, human-checked.

How and when do I get paid?

Payout terms are being finalized now and will be provided in writing during onboarding — clear payout terms before launch, agreed before you accept your first job. We'd rather give you exact terms in writing than a number on a web page that changes later.

Do I have to take every job offered?

No. Every offer shows the appliance, issue, customer photos, address, and window up front, and you accept or decline. Your ratings reflect the work you do — on-time arrivals and first-visit fixes — not how many offers you take.

Can I keep my own customers?

Yes — the network is additional job flow, matched to the areas and appliances you choose. Independent techs and small companies use it to fill gaps in the route and grow into new Broward areas without marketing spend.
