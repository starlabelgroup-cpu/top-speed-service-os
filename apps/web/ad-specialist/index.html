Ad Specialist FAQ

Is this the same as an advertiser account?

No. Advertiser accounts belong to businesses buying Flywheel advertising. Ad Specialist accounts belong to the Top Speed team members who run the ads. Different accounts, different doors, different powers.

What can a specialist approve?

Specialists draft, submit, pause and end campaigns. Final campaign approval — and resuming a paused campaign — stays with the Master, by design.

I forgot my password. What now?

Use "Forgot password" on the Portal sign-in — a reset link goes to your account email. Your username is that same email address; if it was entered wrong at setup, the office corrects it for you.
