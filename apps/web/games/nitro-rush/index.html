TOP SPEED SAFETY — REAL TIPS FOR THE REAL ROAD

Signal every move

Your blinker is how you talk to the road. Signal before every lane change and turn — it gives everyone around you time to react.

Move over for lights

Florida law: when an emergency or service vehicle is stopped or passing with lights on, move over a lane or slow down. It protects the people working roadside — including our techs.

Keep a 3-second gap

Pick a roadside marker; if you pass it less than 3 seconds after the car ahead, you're too close. Double it in rain.

Work zones: slow down

Cones mean people. Fines double in Florida work zones, and the crew has no crumple zone. Slow down and merge early.

Wet roads slide

South Florida rain makes roads slick, especially in the first minutes. Slow down, brake gently, and never use cruise control on wet pavement.

Phones down, eyes up

At 55 mph, five seconds of looking at a phone is a football field of blind driving. Park the phone until you're parked.
