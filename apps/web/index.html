Quick answers

How does the $99 diagnostic work?

It's collected at booking to reserve your window, the tech diagnoses the appliance in person, and if you approve the repair, the full $99 is credited toward the total. You approve a flat quote before any repair work starts.

How fast can you come?

Same-day windows are offered across our 12 core Broward cities when the board allows — pick a window in Smart Booking or call 954-931-7997 and dispatch will tell you the earliest real opening.

Do you service my brand?

Yes — Samsung, LG, Whirlpool, GE, Frigidaire, Maytag, Bosch, KitchenAid and more, including smart/Wi-Fi models and commercial units.
