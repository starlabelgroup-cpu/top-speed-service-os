Quick answers

How does the $99 diagnostic work?

It's collected at booking to reserve your window, the tech diagnoses the appliance in person, and if you approve the repair, the full $99 is credited toward the total. You approve a flat quote before any repair work starts.

How fast can you come?

Same-day windows are offered across our 65-city tri-county service area when the board allows — pick a window in Smart Booking or call 954-931-7997 and dispatch will tell you the earliest real opening.

Do you service my brand?

Yes — Samsung, LG, Whirlpool, GE, Frigidaire, Maytag, Bosch, KitchenAid and more, including smart/Wi-Fi models and commercial units.

Is this the live website?

Yes — this is Top Speed's live platform at topspeedserviceos.com, running on the Top Speed Service OS. Bookings, AI diagnosis, and tracking here are real. Our longtime address topspeedappliance.net keeps running while it moves over to this platform. To book by phone instead: 954-931-7997.
