The reviews engine — the flywheel’s fuel

Every completed job is a marketing asset. The review request fires automatically at job completion inside the Service OS — never left to memory — and every review raises the local visibility that lowers the cost of the next customer.

1

2

3

Review request

An automatic request goes out — timed and framed by Flywheel AI, routed to Google.

4

The review lands on the Business Profile, where future customers and Google both see it.

5

More visibility

Stronger local presence → more calls → more repairs → more reviews. The loop compounds.
