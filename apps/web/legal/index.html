Top Speed Service OS — Terms & Conditions Version 1.0

Effective November 12, 2026 · Between you and Top Speed Appliance LLC ("Top Speed", "we", "us").

1. The service

The Top Speed Service OS is the platform behind Top Speed Appliance's repair service: booking, dispatch by our Service Setters team, technician visits, tracking, billing, and account records for homes, property managers, and commercial accounts across our Broward County service areas. By creating an account or booking service, you agree to these terms.

2. Your account

  • You are responsible for the accuracy of the information on your account — name, contact details, service addresses, and appliance information — and for keeping it current.
  • You are responsible for activity under your account. Keep your password private and tell us promptly if you believe your account has been used without your permission.
  • One account serves you across the Top Speed apps and websites; the app you use depends on your account type (homeowner, property manager, commercial, or provider).

3. Booking and dispatch

  • Appointment windows are two-hour blocks. A booking is reviewed and approved by a human Service Setter before a technician is dispatched — no booking is dispatched by software alone.
  • Please make sure an adult can provide access to the appliance at the appointment, and that the appliance can be reached safely.
  • Job stages, arrival updates, and estimates shown in Track My Tech come from real job events; an estimated arrival time is set by the technician and can change with road and job conditions.

4. Pricing — the diagnostic and your estimate

  • Residential diagnostic: $99, collected at booking and credited toward your repair. The diagnostic covers the technician's inspection and testing on site to identify the actual cause.
  • Commercial accounts are priced by custom quote. The residential diagnostic model does not apply to commercial service.
  • No repair price exists before the diagnostic. On site, the technician names the cause and gives the exact repair estimate; repair work begins only after you approve that estimate.

5. Warranty — 90 days, parts + labor

  • Repairs carry a 90-day parts + labor warranty: it covers the parts we install plus the labor for the specific repair on the invoice, for 90 days from the service date.
  • If the same failure returns inside the warranty, we come back and repair it at no additional labor charge.
  • Not covered: misuse, power surges, water or pest damage, improper installation, normal wear, or work done by others. Commercial equipment may carry additional written terms agreed before work begins.
  • The full warranty terms are printed on your invoice.

6. Speedy, our AI assistant

  • Speedy can answer questions, help you book, and review a photo of an appliance to offer a preliminary read of what might be wrong. Chat and photo triage are preliminary only — a technician confirms the actual cause on site.
  • Speedy never certifies that a repair is safe, never issues a refund (refunds require human approval), and never quotes a repair price before a diagnostic.
  • If a situation sounds safety-critical — gas smell, sparking, smoke, flooding, electric shock — stop using the appliance, get safe, and contact a human professional; Speedy will direct you the same way.

7. Track My Tech and location

Your technician's live location is shown only while the technician is en route to your job, from the technician's own device. It is never shown before that stage and is never generated or estimated by the platform.

8. Referrals and offers

The ApplianceReferral program is governed by its published terms: a $25 service credit per completed referral, $35 each from the 5th through the 9th completed referral, and $50 each from the 10th on, plus one-time milestone bonuses of $100 at 10 and $250 at 25 lifetime completed referrals. Credits are issued after staff approval, apply to future service only, are not cash, and expire 12 months after issuance. A referred friend's separate welcome offer is $25 off a first eligible repair for new registered customers, with the minimums and exclusions stated where the offer appears. Program eligibility and terms are confirmed by the team at booking.
