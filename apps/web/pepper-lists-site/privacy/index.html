Privacy Policy

How Pepper Lists It handles your information — plainly, and only what is true about how the platform actually works.

Effective date: November 22, 2026 · Pepper Lists It ("Pepper Lists") is a product of Top Speed Appliance LLC and runs on the Top Speed Service OS platform. This policy covers pepperlists.com and the Pepper Lists seller and buyer features. The wider Service OS privacy policy also applies to your account and is at topspeedserviceos.com/privacy.

Where your information lives

One account works across the whole Top Speed platform. Your account — name, email address, phone number and password (stored hashed) — lives in the main Top Speed Service OS account system.

Your Pepper Lists data — listings, saved items, storefront, Pepper Lists messages, seller plans, sales and reviews, channel connections, agent grants and action logs — lives in the Pepper Lists database, on the same Service OS server infrastructure. Pepper Lists records refer to your account by its ID; your password is never part of Pepper Lists data.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, phone number and password (stored hashed) when you create an account.
  • Listings — the titles, prices, photos, categories, conditions and descriptions you publish, and your storefront details if you open one.
  • Activity — items you save, messages you exchange with buyers or sellers inside Pepper Lists, sales and reviews, and seller-plan choices.
  • Channel connections — the channels you ask us to connect (for example Facebook, Google Shopping or eBay), the account name or ID you give us for that channel, whether you have granted the Pepper Lists agent access to manage the channel for you, and a log of every action taken on your channels, including the real destination URL when something is posted.
  • Photos and media — photos or videos you choose to upload for a listing or send to Speedy, our AI assistant, for a listing draft.
  • Payment information — payments are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card number.

Signing in with Facebook

If you choose Continue with Facebook, Facebook sends us your name and the email address you choose to share with the app — nothing more. We never receive your Facebook password.

We use that name and email only to sign you in and to match you to your Pepper Lists account by that email address. If no account exists for that email yet, signing in with Facebook creates one under the same free customer terms as any other signup.

Posting to Facebook and other channels

Pepper Lists sellers never connect a personal Facebook account directly. When you confirm a listing and ask us to post it to Facebook, and your agent grant for that channel is on, the post is made through Top Speed's connected Facebook account by Pepper, the Muse AI agent — and the resulting Facebook URL is recorded on your listing and in your action log. You can turn the grant off or disconnect a channel at any time; that stops all future agent actions on that channel.

Public listing details (title, price, photos, availability) are also included in our product catalog feed, which supplies the Pepper Lists catalog on Meta and the other shopping channels you connect, so buyers can find your items there. Checkout always completes on pepperlists.com.

How we use and share information

We use your information to run your listings, storefront, messages and channel connections; to post to the channels you direct us to; to process seller-plan payments and prevent fraud; and to support you when you contact us.

We share information only as needed: with the channels and platforms you ask us to post or list to (such as Meta, Google or eBay), with payment processors such as Stripe, and with the technology providers that run our platform (hosting, email, text messaging and AI assistance). We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices and deletion

You may request a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion of your account and personal data at any time. Deletion covers both your Service OS account and your Pepper Lists data, and the full instructions are on our Data Deletion page. You can also write to us at service@topspeedappliance.net or call (844) 460-7038.

We keep booking, sales and payment records for as long as needed to provide service, honor warranties and meet legal and accounting requirements; those records are covered by the Service OS privacy policy.
