Delete your account and data

One request deletes both your Top Speed Service OS account and everything Pepper Lists holds for you. Here is exactly how, and exactly what happens.

How to request deletion

Use either route — both reach the same team and the same deletion.

1

Email us from your account email

Send an email to service@topspeedappliance.net from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Pepper Lists account". Sending it from your account email is how we know the request is really yours. You can also call (844) 460-7038.

2

If you signed in with Facebook

You can remove Pepper Lists at any time in Facebook under Settings → Apps and Websites. That revokes Facebook's connection to your account. If Meta sends us a data-deletion request on your behalf when you remove the app, we act on it the same way as a direct request. Removing the app alone does not by itself delete the data we hold — send the email in step 1 as well, and both sides are covered.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email address, phone number and sign-in credentials in the Top Speed Service OS account system.
  • Your Pepper Lists data, held in the Pepper Lists database: listings and their photos, saved items, your storefront, Pepper Lists messages, seller-plan records, channel connections, agent grants and the channel action log, and cross-post records.
  • Any name and email address we received through Facebook sign-in.

Posts already published to a channel (for example a Facebook post made through Top Speed's account at your direction) remain on that platform under that platform's own controls; deleting your Pepper Lists data removes our records and links to them here.

What may be kept, and why

Records we are required to keep for legal, tax and accounting reasons — for example completed service bookings, invoices and payment records — are retained under the Top Speed Service OS privacy policy and are not used for marketing. Everything else covered above is deleted or anonymized, including copies in routine backups as those backups cycle out.

Confirmation

We confirm by email when your deletion is complete. If anything about your request is unclear, we reply to the same email address and ask — we never delete the wrong account on a guess.
