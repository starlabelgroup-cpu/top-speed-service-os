      Questions, answered

      How Pepper Lists It actually works — no fine print.

      Does Pepper post without asking me?

      No. Pepper drafts every listing and waits. A listing only goes live after you confirm it in chat — and posting runs through your own connected Facebook account, so it shows up in your Seller app like any listing you made yourself.

      What if Pepper doesn't know the price?

      Then the listing is marked "Needs info" and Pepper asks you. Known regulars come back with their usual prices; anything new waits for your number. Pepper never invents a price.

      Which platforms can I post to today?

      Facebook Marketplace is live. Instagram can be selected and queues until the owner's Instagram account is connected. TikTok, YouTube, eBay and OfferUp are coming soon — they're shown honestly as not live yet.

      Can I book appliance repair here too?

      Yes — Book is one of Pepper Lists' three purposes, alongside Sell and Post. Book Top Speed appliance services with the classic web form (it comes first) or with Speedy AI, track your technician live, keep your appliances in your Garage, and review your repair when it's done.

      Safety & Security

      How Pepper Lists keeps selling, buying and booking straightforward and safe — and what to watch for yourself.

      Verified accounts

      Every seller and buyer on Pepper Lists signs in with a real account. Sellers whose email is verified carry an Email verified badge on their listings, and sellers who turned on 2-step sign-in carry a 2-step on badge — both badges only ever appear when they are true. You can also see how long a seller has been a member and how many sales they have completed here.

      2-step sign-in

      Your Pepper Lists account is your Top Speed account. Turn on 2-step sign-in (Layer 2) from your account settings and every sign-in asks for a one-time code on top of your password — so a leaked password alone cannot get anyone into your listings, bookings or reviews.

      Reporting works

      Every listing has a Report this listing button. Pick a reason, add a note if you like, and the report goes straight to the Top Speed office queue — a person reads every report. Staff can remove comments and listings that break the rules. If someone pressures you to move a conversation off Pepper Lists, that is exactly what the report button is for.

      Safe buying & selling

      Meet in a public place in daylight when you can, bring a friend for big items, and check the item before money changes hands. Pepper Lists will never ask you to pay by gift card or wire transfer, and neither will Top Speed. If a "buyer" sends a check for more than the price and asks for change back, it is a scam — walk away and report the listing.

      Booking through Pepper Lists

      Booking a repair here connects you to the real Top Speed Service OS: the classic web form is the primary booking path, Speedy AI sits below it and always summarizes your details for your confirmation before anything reaches the Service Setters office. Booking payments run through Stripe. The residential diagnostic is $99, collected at booking and credited toward an approved repair; repair labor is a flat $210 and installation a flat $150, parts at a written quote — no work ever starts without your approved written quote.

      Reviews you can trust

      Service reviews on Pepper Lists can only be written after a completed repair, and seller reviews only after a completed sale recorded here. Ratings start from real reviews only — when a seller or a service has no reviews yet, the page says exactly that instead of showing stars nobody earned.
