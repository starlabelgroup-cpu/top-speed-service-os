    How it works

    Two ways to use Pepper Lists — sell your products, or book a service. Either way you stay in control the whole way: Pepper prepares, you approve.

    Selling — four steps to a live listing

    1

    Snap a photo

    Point your camera at the item — a box of ackee, a mango tree, a stack of produce. One photo is enough to start.

    2

    AI drafts the listing

    Pepper recognizes the item and drafts the title, category, condition and description, with a confidence score. Regulars are matched to their usual prices automatically.

    3

    You review

    Check the draft, fix anything, set the price if it's something new. If a price is missing, Pepper asks — it never invents one.

    4

    Pepper posts it

    Only after you confirm in chat does Pepper post the listing through your connected account. You get the live link back.

    Booking — four steps to a fixed appliance

    1

    Say what's wrong

    Open the booking form or tell Speedy. Snap a photo of the appliance or its model plate — Speedy reads it and starts the booking with you.

    2

    Confirm the visit

    The Service Setters confirm your details and time window. The residential diagnostic is $99, collected at booking and credited toward your repair.

    3

    Track your tech

    On the day, follow your technician live on the map — and message the office any time, right from here.

    4

    Review the repair

    When the job is done, rate it in Pepper Lists Reviews — Speedy answers every review personally.

    Questions, answered

    How Pepper Lists It actually works — no fine print.

    Does Pepper post without asking me?

    No. Pepper drafts every listing and waits. A listing only goes live after you confirm it in chat — and posting runs through your own connected Facebook account, so it shows up in your Seller app like any listing you made yourself.

    What if Pepper doesn't know the price?

    Then the listing is marked "Needs info" and Pepper asks you. Known regulars come back with their usual prices; anything new waits for your number. Pepper never invents a price.

    Which platforms can I post to today?

    Facebook Marketplace is live. Instagram can be selected and queues until the owner's Instagram account is connected. TikTok, YouTube, eBay and OfferUp are coming soon — they're shown honestly as not live yet.

    Can I book appliance repair here too?

    Yes — that's the other half of Pepper Lists. It's the Top Speed customer app for both sides: sell your products, and book Top Speed appliance services. Book with the classic web form or with Speedy, track your technician live, keep your appliances in your Garage, and review your repair when it's done.

    How do I advertise inside Pepper Lists?

    Sign in, open the Advertise lane, and send your ad. The Top Speed office reviews every ad before it runs — submitting is free, and nothing is charged or shown without office approval. Approved ads appear in the Customer Ads section here and can rotate in the Flywheel mini banner across Top Speed sites.
