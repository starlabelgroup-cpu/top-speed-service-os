Top Speed Sales OS

The sales platform inside the Service OS. Its first vendor is Top Speed Appliances — the used-appliance shop at topspeedappliances.com. Staff enter real units here as drafts and activate them; the storefront sells only ACTIVE products. Orders are priced from the stored catalog on the server and paid through the platform's Stripe checkout — when Stripe is not configured, an order saves as pending payment with no checkout link, and this console says so.
