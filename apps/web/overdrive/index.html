The Network

How the Top Speed network works

One booking, one connected system. Every job follows the same six steps, whether a Company Tech or a vetted network provider turns the wrench.

1

You book

Use the classic web form at /book — the primary booking path — or tell Speedy AI what is wrong, with a photo if you have one. Speedy summarizes everything for your confirmation before anything is sent.

2

Service Setters dispatch

Your request lands with Service Setters, the dispatch heart of Top Speed. They confirm the details and assign the right technician for the job and your area.

3

The right tech rolls

Jobs go to Top Speed Company Techs or vetted independent providers in the Top Speed network — matched by skill, coverage and availability.

4

Track My Tech

Follow your technician on the way with live tracking, so you know who is coming and when they will arrive at your door.

5

Repaired, under warranty

The work is completed at your home and every repair is backed by our 90-day parts & labor warranty.

6

Review & refer

Tell us how it went, then refer friends and neighbors — completed referrals earn credits toward your next service (real numbers below).
