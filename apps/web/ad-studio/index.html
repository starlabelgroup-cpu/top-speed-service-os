Top Speed Ads

Our own advertising system — the Ad Studio of Flywheel AI Advertising — on the same Service OS as booking and dispatch. Campaigns are built from the Media OS creative library and the house poster and scenario images, send traffic to our own landing pages, and run as placements on our own platform surfaces — the flagship footer banner rotation. The Streaming Ads channel adds Google-style text and display ads aimed at streaming TV providers, reviewed here as exact previews. Every lead and booking a campaign produces is counted by its source tag, and ad clicks are now traced end to end: first-touch attribution (UTM tags and the Google, Meta and Microsoft click IDs) rides along with bookings, and the banner feed counts its own raw serves and clicks. Speedy can draft campaign copy — drafts always land in the owner's approval queue, never live. Nothing goes live without the owner's approval.

Pepper Agent — System Operations Manager Manager

Pepper Agent (Muse AI) runs the studio day to day: builds campaigns from the creative library, wires the placements, tracks the source-tag results, and queues everything for approval. Pepper never launches a campaign and never spends a dollar — there is no outside spend in this system at all.

Cost per won job

Every campaign joined to what it actually produced: attributed leads carried through the CRM pipeline to won jobs, and the real money collected on those jobs — card payments plus recorded receipts, never an estimate dressed up as revenue. Impressions and clicks are the in-house banner counters, all time (they are running counters, not a windowed log). Cost per won job divides only the spend a person records on the campaign — record or correct it in the campaign's detail view (open the campaign, use Recorded spend). It is never estimated and never imported: Google / Meta / Microsoft spend lives in those platforms. A campaign with no recorded spend says "No cost recorded"; a campaign with recorded spend but no won jobs yet shows the spend with the per-won figure as —.

Ad serving — provider connections

The AdStream layer: the external ad-serving providers Top Speed Ads can serve through, next to the in-house display feed that serves our own surfaces today.
