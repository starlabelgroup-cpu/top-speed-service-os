Questions, answered

The things South Florida homeowners ask us most — straight answers, no fine print.

How does the $99 diagnostic work?

The $99 diagnostic is collected when you book a residential visit. The technician diagnoses the appliance and gives you a written quote. If you approve the repair, the $99 is credited toward your total. If you decline the repair, the $99 is still due for the visit.

What will my repair cost?

Repair labor is a flat $210, plus any parts and materials — all itemized on a written quote you approve before work begins. Installation labor is a flat $150. No work is ever done without an approved written quote.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. Completed repairs carry a 90-day warranty on parts and labor, and our technicians are licensed & EPA certified.

How do I book?

Book online through the Service OS booking page, chat with Speedy AI using the Speedy button on this page (you can describe the problem or send a photo), or call 954-931-7997, Monday–Friday 8am–7pm.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve Broward, Palm Beach and Miami-Dade counties — including Fort Lauderdale, Plantation, Sunrise, Coral Springs, Pompano Beach, Boca Raton, Miami and the surrounding cities listed above.

When do I pay?

The $99 diagnostic is collected at booking. The repair balance is due on completion, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
