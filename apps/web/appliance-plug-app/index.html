How a job flows
Snap tag→ Speedy reads it→ You confirm→ Parts Finder→ Order queued

Every read is tech-confirmed before an order exists — wrong model means wrong parts, so Speedy never orders on its own.

Snap the Tag

Point the camera at the model plate — usually inside the door frame, on the back panel, or behind the kick plate. Speedy reads it and pulls out:

1Brand & appliance typeRefrigerator, washer, dryer, range, dishwasher and more.
2Model & serial numbersRead with a confidence score — low-confidence reads get flagged for a second look.
3You confirm or editEvery field is editable. Tag too damaged to read? Type it in, or Ask Speedy in free text.
Confirm before ordering — a wrong model number means wrong parts. Speedy flags what it's unsure about; the final call is always yours, the licensed tech.
Open in Service OS

Opens the Top Speed Service OS, where the live camera read runs under your tech sign-in.

Parts Finder

Once the model is confirmed, pick the symptom and Speedy returns the part categories that fit — each with fitment notes and what to verify first on the unit.

1Pick the symptomNot cooling, won't drain, no heat, loud spin — plain-language symptom chips.
2Get part categoriesFor example: evaporator fan motor, defrost heater, defrost sensor, thermistor — matched to the model's platform.
3Verify, then queue the orderCheck the verify-first note on the truck, set quantity and urgency, and send it to the order queue.
Exact OEM part numbers and prices are confirmed by Parts Supply OS at order time. This app never invents a part number or a price — if it isn't confirmed, it doesn't show.
Open in Service OS

Parts Finder runs inside the Service OS under your tech sign-in.

Orders

Parts you queue land in the order queue with quantity, an optional Top Speed job reference, and urgency — standard, same-day, or emergency. Routing is automatic:

1Appliance Plus Parts Supply — firstQueued as a Parts Supply OS draft for the home team to file.
2Other distributor — secondUsed when the partner can't fill it.
Order statuses — honest, tap by tap
DraftBuilt, not sent
Sent to Parts Supply OSFiled with the home team
OrderedPlaced with the vendor
ReceivedOn the truck / at the shop

Statuses only move when someone really taps them — no fake "ordered" stamps.
