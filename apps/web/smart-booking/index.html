The automated job lifecycle

Every request — from any channel — flows through the same ten steps on the Service OS.

1 · Request

Speedy AI receives your request — chat, photo, video or form.

2 · Customer match

The CRM creates or matches your customer record.

3 · Service ticket

AI prepares a structured request: appliance, symptoms, location, evidence.

4 · Booking validation

Scheduling checks territory, technician capacity and availability.

5 · Confirmation

You confirm the offered appointment — nothing shows as confirmed before that.

6 · Dispatch

Service Setters assign the work order under hybrid dispatch rules.

7 · Field service

The technician records diagnosis, work performed, parts and evidence.

8 · Estimate approval

You review and accept the flat estimate before repair work starts.

9 · Closeout

Payment, invoice and service documentation are recorded.

10 · Follow-up

The CRM initiates satisfaction, review and retention follow-up.

Booking integrity

An appointment is never displayed as confirmed until the scheduling system has verified availability and you — or an authorized approver — have accepted it. Estimates and charges follow explicit authorization rules. That is a promise built into the system, not a slogan.
