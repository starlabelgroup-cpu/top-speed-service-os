Built for commercial accounts

Appliance Pro runs on an Appliance Pro Commercial Account under Top Speed Appliance LLC — custom-quoted per facility. The residential $99 diagnostic doesn't apply commercially.

Equipment registry

Every unit on file with an automatic asset tag (TS-EQ-XXXX), brand, model, serial number, warranty status and health status — across all your locations.

Bulk requests, sorted by Speedy

Type or paste the day's requests in plain words. Speedy AI sorts them into separate work orders, and the Service Setters confirm every one before dispatch.

Service activity

Every request and every visit in one place — what was reported, what the technician found, and a photo report when the job closes.

Priority where it matters

Equipment marked critical — and top-tier accounts — move first in the dispatch queue automatically.

One monthly invoice

All work across all locations rolls into a single consolidated monthly invoice. No per-job paperwork chasing.

Three agreement tiers

Essential, Priority and Total Care service agreements, priced by custom quote for your facility and equipment mix.

Who it's for

Restaurants, hotels, laundromats, healthcare, offices, retail and facilities across Broward, Palm Beach & Miami-Dade — the 65 cities Top Speed serves, from Fort Lauderdale to Coral Springs.
