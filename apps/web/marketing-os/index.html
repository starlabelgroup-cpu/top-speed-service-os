How an engagement works

Four steps, the same loop the platform runs for its own growth.

STEP 1

Intake

Tell us the business, the market and the goal. Your intake lands in the Service OS with a reference, and the agency manager reviews it personally.

STEP 2

Build

Pages are drafted in the Page Studio from the taxonomy — canonical URLs, structured sections, metadata — and held in review until approved.

STEP 3

Launch

Approved pages publish on the canonical domain, wired to Speedy AI booking and the CRM so every lead is attributed end to end.

STEP 4

Optimize

We judge pages and campaigns on qualified leads and booked jobs — and expand coverage only where the service team can actually deliver.
